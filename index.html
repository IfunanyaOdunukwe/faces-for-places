Features

  • Sky: dawn, day, dusk and night from your sunrise and sunset, with twilight as long as it is where you are
  • Lighting: follow the sun, one time of day always, or your own times
  • Weather: rain, snow, thunderstorms, sleet and clouds, each animated
  • Weather source: Garmin, Open-Meteo or OpenWeather (your key), for your location or a custom one
  • Wake animation: every wrist raise, at most every 15 minutes, or off
  • Goal fireworks: steps, floors and intensity minutes
  • Status icons: charging, and phone out of range
  • Slots: up to three, from heart rate, steps, battery, Body Battery, stress, temperature, UV index, sunrise, sunset, calories, floors, intensity minutes, Pulse Ox, respiration, sleep score, altitude, recovery time and alarms
  • Time and date: 12 or 24-hour; three date styles, or none

  • Settings: in the phone app or on the watch
  • Languages: English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Arabic, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional)
  • Screens: AMOLED and MIP, with palettes for each

FAQ

How do I install it?

In the Connect IQ Store app on your phone, search for "Faces for Places", choose Install, and it goes to your watch at the next sync. On the watch, pick it like any other watch face. When it asks, allow location: it picks your sunrise, sunset and weather (see Privacy).

What's the difference between Lite and the full version?

Only the cities. Lite is free and has London, with every sky, weather, animation and setting. The full version (£2.99, coming soon) adds New York, Paris, Manchester and Giza, and Nearest to me. Try Lite first to see how it looks on your watch.

How do I change the settings?

On the phone: in the Connect IQ Store app, open the face under your watch's watch faces and choose Settings. On the watch: open the watch face's menu and choose Customise. Both have the same settings in the same order: the everyday ones first, then Display and battery, Sky and lighting, and Weather source.

I changed a setting on the phone and it went back

Garmin's phone app can lose a change made inside a section (Display and battery, Sky and lighting, Weather source) if you go back without saving. Press Save before leaving the section, or change it on the watch (Customise), which always keeps it.

How does Nearest to me work?

In the full version, Nearest to me (the default Place) shows whichever city is closest to you, using your phone's location or the watch's last GPS fix. It checks again when you raise your wrist, so it changes when you travel. Pick a city under Place to keep that one.

Which weather source should I use?
  • Garmin (the default): uses the least battery, and works with the phone away for a while. It's said to not be as accurate as the others though
  • Open-Meteo: free, nothing to set up.
  • OpenWeather: needs your own free API key from openweathermap.org, typed into the OpenWeather API key setting. A new key can take a few hours to start working.

The web sources are fetched through your phone every 15, 30 or 60 minutes, as you choose, so the phone needs to be nearby with Bluetooth on.

The weather on the watch isn't what I see outside

Weather reaches the watch through your phone, so it can be a little behind, and Garmin's reading comes from a nearby weather station. A web reading more than 6 hours old stops showing rain. If it's often wrong for you, try another weather source.

What are the little icons above the time?

A plug means the watch is charging. A crossed-out phone means your phone is out of range, so the weather won't update until it's back. You can turn either off under Display and battery.

Does it use much battery?

The defaults (Garmin weather for where you are) use the least. Web weather, a custom weather location and the wake animation on every wrist raise use more; the settings mark them with "more battery".

When do the fireworks go off?

The next time you raise your wrist after reaching your step or floors goal (once a day each) or your intensity-minutes goal (once a week). You can turn them off in the settings.

Which watches does it work on?

Round Garmin watches with Connect IQ 5 or 6 (Forerunner, fenix, epix, Enduro, Venu, vivoactive, Instinct AMOLED and others), plus the Venu Sq 2 and Venu X1. The store page lists every model.
