Your watch code

You'll find it in Lite's settings: on the watch under Customise (the first row), or on your phone in the Connect IQ Store app, under the face's Settings. Check it carefully: the unlock code only works on the watch this code came from.

What happens next

  1. You pay on a Stripe page. Stripe sells the code for me, through its Onelink service, and handles your payment and tax. Your price may be shown in your own currency, and your statement will show LINK.COM.
  2. Your unlock code appears straight after paying. Save it: your receipt email doesn't include it. If you ever lose it, email me your watch code and receipt and I'll send it again.
  3. On your phone, type it into Lite's settings under Unlock code and press Save, then sync your watch.
